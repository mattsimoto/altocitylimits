Original Medieval Asset Library

These are the actual illuminated-manuscript images generated for Alto City Limits—the dragon, snail, manicules, vines, initials, diagrams, bestiary figures and borders from the approved preview. They are stored in one optimized transparent image and exposed as tight responsive crops so the artwork keeps its original proportions and details instead of being redrawn or distorted.

The earlier simplified SVG approximations have been retired. The Dark Ages page and this library now render the original generated artwork directly, without multiply blending, recoloring, or shape reconstruction.
