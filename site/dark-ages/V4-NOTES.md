# Dark Ages v4 WIP

The v4 pass keeps the spacious Editorial-derived layout and adds manuscript marginalia rather than compressing the content.

Changes in this pass:
- decorative bestiary figures move into the outer page margins on wide screens;
- marginalia automatically disappears as the viewport narrows;
- restrained manicules and ornamental rules add manuscript rhythm without changing the content hierarchy;
- the biography/support copy gains illuminated drop caps;
- the four display-mode icons remain small and utility-level;
- all primary content stays in the responsive reading grid.

This remains a work in progress and is not the locked production theme.
